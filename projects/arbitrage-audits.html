Outcome-Free Arbitrage Audits and Coherence Repairs for LLM Forecasters

Explore Research

LLM forecasters output probability estimates that look authoritative but are routinely incoherent - assigning P(A) + P(¬A) > 1, breaking monotonicity between nested events, or producing conjunctions whose probability exceeds their marginals. The usual way to check a forecaster is to wait for outcomes and score Brier loss, but that takes months and excludes any question whose ground truth never resolves. This work develops audits that detect incoherence without waiting for any outcome, plus lightweight repairs that bring the forecaster back to coherent predictions.

  • Outcome-free arbitrage audits. I construct query portfolios whose probabilities, taken at face value, expose the forecaster to a sure loss - a Dutch book against its own predictions. Detection is purely internal: only the model's own outputs are needed, so the audit runs at inference time on any forecaster, frontier or fine-tuned, and on any question family regardless of resolution horizon.
  • Coherence repairs. Given an incoherent prediction set, I project it onto the probability simplex (and onto monotonicity and conjunction-marginal constraints) under a total-variation objective. The simplex repair is closed-form; the structural constraints reduce to a small quadratic program, so the repair composes with the audit in a single forward pass and preserves the forecaster's information about which event is most likely.
  • Why outcome-free matters. Calibration-style audits need ground truth, which biases monitoring toward near-term resolvable questions and leaves long-horizon or never-resolving forecasts unchecked. Outcome-free audits scale to any horizon, making them suitable for continuous monitoring of deployed forecasters in production rather than as after-the-fact post-mortems.
